# Unaffiliated Research Record

## Overview

This repository serves as a public evidence ledger supporting recognition of unaffiliated independent research as a measurable and verifiable category of human achievement.

The project documents DOI-registered research output generated without institutional funding, university affiliation, dedicated research staff, or proprietary research infrastructure.

Its purpose is not to argue that independent researchers are superior to institutional researchers. Rather, it seeks to demonstrate that research conducted outside formal institutions can be measured, verified, documented, and compared using transparent public records.

---

## Why This Repository Exists

World record institutions recognize thousands of measurable achievements across sports, endurance, collections, craftsmanship, hobbies, and other human activities.

However, there is currently no widely recognized category dedicated to measuring large-scale research output produced under fully independent conditions.

This repository supports the idea that unaffiliated independent research deserves consideration as a distinct and measurable category because:

* Research output can be publicly verified through DOI registrations.
* Performance can be measured over a defined period.
* Results are theoretically challengeable and breakable.
* Independent researchers operate under a unique set of constraints that differ from institutional environments.

---

## Proposed Category

**Most DOI-Registered Research Outputs Produced by an Unaffiliated Independent Researcher Within One Calendar Year**

### Proposed Characteristics

* Measurable
* Verifiable
* Challengeable
* Breakable
* Publicly documented

### Proposed Conditions

* No institutional affiliation requirement
* No university employment requirement
* No dedicated research staff requirement
* No external grant requirement
* Output must be publicly attributable to a named individual
* Output must be verifiable through DOI registration and public records

## Purpose

The objective of this repository is to encourage discussion about how independent research achievements can be documented and recognized using transparent, publicly verifiable metrics.

The repository does not seek preferential treatment or exemption from verification standards.

It advocates that independent research conducted without institutional support represents a legitimate and measurable form of intellectual achievement.
