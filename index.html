The Unaffiliated Research Record

A Public Evidence Ledger for Independent Research Achievement

Proposed Category: Most DOI-Registered Research Outputs Produced by an Unaffiliated Independent Researcher Within One Calendar Year

Research is one of humanity's most important activities. Yet there is currently no widely recognized world-record category measuring high-volume research output generated without institutional funding, university affiliation, dedicated research staff, or proprietary research infrastructure.

Purpose

This repository exists to document a measurable form of intellectual achievement that currently lacks dedicated recognition within major world-record frameworks.

Independent research is conducted worldwide by inventors, farmers, citizen scientists, open-source contributors, self-funded scholars, and unaffiliated researchers. Knowledge creation is not exclusive to universities or corporations.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate that independent research output can be:

  • Measured
  • Verified
  • Documented
  • Compared
  • Challenged
  • Potentially surpassed

Current Status

On 20 April 2026, a proposal was submitted to Guinness World Records for consideration of a new category:

"Unaffiliated Independent Researcher with Most DOIs in a Year"

The proposal proceeded to a detailed evaluation stage involving questions related to measurability, achievability, verifiability, and breakability.

This ledger serves as a permanent public archive documenting the rationale for recognizing independent research output as a measurable category of human achievement.
